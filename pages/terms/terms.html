Explore with care and respect.

Acceptance of these terms

By accessing or using HobbyHub, users will be expected to follow these Terms & Conditions and any policies referenced here. If a user does not agree with the applicable terms, they should not use the platform.

Where required by law, users will be asked to provide clear consent before using relevant services.

2

Accounts and eligibility

Some future HobbyHub features may require an account. Users will be responsible for providing accurate information, keeping their sign-in credentials secure, and activity carried out through their account.

Account registration is not active in the current prototype. Eligibility requirements, including any minimum age, must be finalized before launch.

3

Acceptable use

Users are expected to act respectfully and lawfully. They must not harass others, post unlawful or harmful material, impersonate another person, interfere with platform security, or misuse the service.

When moderation and reporting features are implemented, HobbyHub’s handling process and any available enforcement actions should be described clearly.

4

User content and feedback

Users may be able to submit messages, feedback, profile details, or other content through future features. Users should only submit content they have the right to share and should avoid including sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

The current Contact Us form is a front-end demonstration. Submissions are not currently stored or delivered to the HobbyHub admin dashboard. Any future content licence, retention, or removal rules must be documented before launch.

5

Activities and third-party services

HobbyHub may display hobby-related classes, events, training, or links to services operated by others. Details, availability, fees, cancellations, and safety requirements may be controlled by the relevant organizer or provider.

Before any booking or enrollment feature goes live, the platform must explain its role, payment handling (if any), cancellation rules, and the responsibilities of users and providers.

6

Availability and changes

We aim to make HobbyHub useful and reliable, but cannot promise uninterrupted availability. Features, content, or parts of the platform may change as the product develops.

Material changes to these terms should be reflected in an updated version with a revised date and any notice required by applicable law.

7

Disclaimers and liability

HobbyHub is currently a prototype, and its live service commitments, warranties, and liability limits have not yet been finalized. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the final terms should clearly explain service limitations and the respective responsibilities of the platform and its users.

Nothing in these draft terms is intended to exclude rights or protections that cannot legally be excluded.

8

Contact and updates

Questions about these terms may be submitted through the Contact Us page once its message handling is connected. In the current prototype, that form does not send or save messages.

Before launch, HobbyHub should publish an appropriate contact channel and identify the operating entity and governing law where required.
