THE SHORT VERSION

Your information deserves care.

This page explains our intended privacy approach in clear terms. HobbyHub is currently a front-end prototype: account creation, contact messages, and other form submissions are not connected to a live backend and are not saved or sent by the current demo pages.

ON THIS PAGE

Information and formsHow information may be usedSharing and securityYour choicesChildren’s privacyPolicy updates
1

Information and forms

When the relevant features are connected, HobbyHub may ask for information such as your name, email address, account details, preferences, or messages you choose to send through the platform.

For the current prototype: the Sign Up and Contact Us forms are front-end demonstrations only. They do not create a real account or deliver and store a submitted contact message.

2

How information may be used

When implemented, information may be used to provide and maintain HobbyHub features, help users discover hobbies and activities, respond to support requests, improve the experience, and protect the platform from misuse.

We intend to use information only for clear, relevant purposes and to explain those purposes when information is collected.

3

Sharing and security

HobbyHub is being designed with privacy and appropriate access controls in mind. Future implementation should limit access to personal information to authorized people and use suitable technical and organizational safeguards.

We do not claim that live storage, security controls, or third-party data-sharing arrangements are active in this prototype. Those details must be confirmed and documented before production launch.

4

Your choices

When accounts and data-management features become available, we intend to provide clear ways to review or update relevant account information and to request assistance with personal information, subject to applicable requirements.

For now, the prototype does not provide a live account or stored submission to update or delete.

5

Children’s privacy

HobbyHub should not knowingly collect personal information from children without the permissions or safeguards required by applicable law. Before launch, the platform’s age requirements and any additional protections for younger users must be clearly defined.

6

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as HobbyHub’s features and technical setup evolve. Any published version should reflect the platform’s actual practices, explain material changes, and show when it was last updated.

A NOTE ABOUT THIS VERSION
